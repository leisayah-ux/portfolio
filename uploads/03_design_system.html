06 — Design Principles
01
Light base, one moment of weight
The site stays open and readable. One section — the bold dark panel — earns all the visual gravity. Contrast only works when it's earned.
02
Forest as punctuation, not wallpaper
The accent appears on metrics, tags, CTAs, and active states. Nowhere decorative. Its restraint is what gives it power when it shows up.
03
Numbers are the headline
+40% return visitor conversion. +30% quote submissions. These are your proof points — large, early, impossible to scroll past.
04
Earn every scroll
Recruiters scan in 30 seconds. Hiring managers go deep. Every section needs a strong headline for the scan and substance for the read.
05
Space is confidence
Crowding signals insecurity. Generous whitespace signals someone who doesn't need to prove everything at once. Let the work breathe — it's strong enough.

08 — What to Avoid
✕ Multiple accent colours — forest loses power the moment something else competes
✕ Gradients, glow effects, or anything that reads "SaaS landing page 2021"
✕ Illustrations or decorative avatars — they soften the authority signal
✕ Bullet-list case studies — narrative paragraphs, not résumé formatting
✕ Generic fonts: Inter, Roboto, system-ui — they read as default, not designed
✕ More than one bold dark section — the contrast moment only works once per page
